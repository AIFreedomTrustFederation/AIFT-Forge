AIFT-Forge owns deterministic, offline-first presentation geometry, not doctrine or authorization.

Suggested pure functions: fibonacci(n) for bounded nonnegative integers; goldenRatio()=(1+sqrt(5))/2; goldenAngleDegrees()=360/φ²; phyllotaxis(count, angleDegrees, spacing) returning points at radius=spacing*sqrt(index). Validate finite inputs, counts, bounds and overflow. Test n=0,1,2,10, golden-angle tolerance, determinism, malformed inputs, and no network dependency.
